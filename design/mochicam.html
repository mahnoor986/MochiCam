  • flash · white overlay 0 → 1 → 0, 500 ms (800 ms with flash on)
  • hearts · 12 hearts, 1.6 s, rise 190, drift ±60, stagger 0–250 ms
  • toast · spring in from +16 y and 0.9 scale, hold 1.9 s
  • screen enter · fade + 10 rise, 340 ms ease
